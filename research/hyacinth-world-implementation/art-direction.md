# Psychedelic etching

Keep the photographic dotted hyacinth, personal interface, 44-viewport journey and centered compositions. Replace the repeated glossy ribbons and crescent confetti with an environment whose material is fine engraved light.

The support system has three scales: large asymmetric folded contour fields, small delicate orbit knots, and the existing dotted currents. Open spaces inside the large forms frame the flower; their crossings and fine line density create depth without covering its silhouette in plastic surfaces. Violet and pearl dominate, with turquoise and warm copper confined to selected ridges.

| Passage | Composition and transition |
| --- | --- |
| Opening, 0–10 | Original entry, then broad open arcs emerge behind and beside the right-hand flower. Their contour spacing breathes as the first petal opens. |
| Central passage, 10–24 | The arcs fold into tighter, oblique fans around the centered macro view. Scroll pulls their threads past one another; crossings form interference patterns. |
| Underside, 24–30 | Long dark-indigo loops, sparse pearl ridges and copper highlights. Multiple layers remain present with less luminosity. |
| Lower bloom, 30–38 | Forms elongate and sweep diagonally. Small orbit knots and broken particle halos echo their direction. |
| Release, 38–44 | A wider open aureole surrounds the centered whole flower. The outer contours flare; the central silhouette stays clear. |

Native scroll controls folding, shape and placement. Slow ambient motion changes the light and flexes the contours at rest. Reversal reconstructs the same composition. Reduced motion freezes large movement. Text and navigation remain fixed; no new visible copy is introduced.

Acceptance: inspect actual early, central, quiet and wide compositions; no obvious repeated crescent shapes, no broad opaque band covering the flower, substantial supporting activity throughout, and a clear distinction between the quiet interval and the peaks.
