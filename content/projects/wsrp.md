Mineral formation is driven by chaotic factors that are difficult to directly analyse. This project simulates how fluids such as copper and gold, under a high temperature/pressure environment, flow around obstacles, and models their velocities and movements using the Navier-Stokes equations. It does so through a series of mathematical functions, before plotting visualizations with the returned information. Two such visualizations are provided, a 3D animated box and a 2D interactive interface which allows for more user control. These tools have high potential for use in further investigation into geochemical reactions under the simulated conditions, as such, methods for doing so are discussed. This project was completed in the summer of 2025 as part of the Wolfram Summer Research program.
